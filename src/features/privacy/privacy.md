# Privacy

Last updated October 28, 2026

Ashfolk is a free game that runs in your browser. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no cookies. Your worlds and settings stay on your device. This page lists every place where data about you goes, and why.

## Who is responsible

Ashfolk is made by Vlad Svidskiy, an individual developer. For any question about this page or your data, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/imsvidskiy/ashfolk/issues) or write to [@imsvidskiy](https://github.com/imsvidskiy) on GitHub.

## What stays on your device

| What | Where | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saved worlds and their journals | Your browser's IndexedDB | Until you delete the world or clear site data |
| Graphics quality, labels and power settings | Your browser's local storage | Until you clear site data |

None of this is sent to me or anyone else. Delete a world from the start screen, or remove everything by clearing site data for ashfolk.com in your browser.

## What leaves your device

### Hosting by Cloudflare

Cloudflare serves ashfolk.com. Like any web host, its servers receive your IP address, browser and device type, the address you open and the time, so they can deliver the game and block attacks. Request logs and analytics are turned off for the site; I see only total traffic, such as requests per country, and never single visitors or IP addresses. Cloudflare handles this data under its [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) and may process it outside your country.

The legal basis is legitimate interest: the game can't reach you without it.

### Star count from GitHub

The start screen, the game and Settings show how many stars Ashfolk has on GitHub. Your browser asks the GitHub API for that number directly, so GitHub receives your IP address and browser type. The request carries no cookies or GitHub sign-in. GitHub handles it under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The legal basis is legitimate interest in showing the project's current star count.

### Links you follow

GitHub and Sponsor open github.com in a new tab, where GitHub's privacy statement applies. If you sponsor Ashfolk, GitHub handles the payment. I see your GitHub username and tier unless you sponsor privately, and never your payment details.

The game loads no other third-party scripts, fonts or images.

## What Ashfolk doesn't do

- Ask for your name, email or any other personal information
- Set cookies or use tracking pixels, fingerprinting or analytics
- Show ads, sell data or build profiles

Ashfolk collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete personal data, object to how it's used, or complain to the data protection authority where you live. Since I hold no personal data about players, there's usually nothing for me to hand over or delete. For data held by Cloudflare or GitHub, contact them directly, or ask me and I'll help.
